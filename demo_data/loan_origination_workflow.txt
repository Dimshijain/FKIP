1. LOAN APPLICATION STAGES
The Loan Origination System (APP-LOAN) processes consumer personal loans, mortgages, and commercial lines of credit through five automated pipeline phases:
- Stage 1: Application Intake & Document OCR Parsing (Paystubs, W2s, Tax Returns).
- Stage 2: Credit Bureau Pull (Experian, TransUnion, Equifax API Integration).
- Stage 3: Automated Underwriting Engine (FICO Score, Debt-to-Income Calculation).
- Stage 4: Risk-Based Pricing & Interest Rate Calculation.
- Stage 5: Digital Contract E-Signing & Automatic Account Disbursement.

2. UNDERWRITING DECISIONING RULES
- Auto-Approve: FICO Score >= 720 AND Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio <= 35% AND zero bankruptcy records within 7 years.
- Manual Underwriter Review: FICO Score between 620 and 719 OR DTI between 36% and 48%.
- Auto-Decline: FICO Score < 620 OR DTI > 48% OR active tax lien records.

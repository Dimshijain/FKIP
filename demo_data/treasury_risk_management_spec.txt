1. LIQUIDITY COVERAGE RATIO (LCR)
The Treasury Risk Manager (APP-TREASURY) continuously calculates the bank's LCR to ensure High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) exceed 30-day projected net cash outflows during stress scenarios.

2. INTEREST RATE RISK IN THE BANKING BOOK (IRRBB)
The engine monitors net interest income (NII) sensitivity against parallel and non-parallel yield curve shocks (+200bps, -200bps). Automated interest rate swaps (IRS) are proposed when NII variance exceeds 5%.

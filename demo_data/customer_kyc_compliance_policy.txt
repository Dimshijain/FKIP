1. OVERVIEW & REGULATORY DIRECTIVES
This document details the Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification procedures enforced across all core banking onboarding portals in compliance with FinCEN, FATF, and GDPR regulations.

2. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION PROGRAM (CIP)
During digital account creation, customers must submit:
- Government-Issued Photo ID (Passport, Driver's License, National Identity Card).
- Proof of Address (Utility Bill, Bank Statement dated within 90 days).
- Biometric Selfie Liveness Check matching ID photo (minimum 98% confidence score).
- Tax Identification Number (SSN / ITIN / EIN).

3. RISK SCORING & ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE (EDD)
Every customer profile is assigned a dynamic Risk Rating (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH):
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) automatically trigger High Risk / EDD review.
- High Risk accounts undergo mandatory quarterly activity re-assessments.
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) are generated when transaction amounts exceed $10,000 USD within 24 hours without legitimate commercial purpose.

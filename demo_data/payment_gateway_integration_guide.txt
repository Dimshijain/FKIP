1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
The Real-Time Payments Gateway (APP-PAY) processes high-throughput retail and wholesale payment instructions across domestic and cross-border payment networks. The service supports ISO 20022 XML standards including pacs.008 (Financial Institution Transfer), pacs.009 (Financial Institution Credit Transfer), and camt.053 (Bank-to-Customer Statement).

2. TRANSACTION WORKFLOW & LATENCY SLA
- Pre-Validation: Validate IBAN formatting, account active status, and positive ledger balance. (SLA: < 15ms)
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Screening: Real-time sanction list check against OFAC and EU lists. (SLA: < 25ms)
- Ledger Debit/Credit Execution: Two-phase commit lock on debtor account ledger. (SLA: < 20ms)
- Clearinghouse Transmission: Encrypted TLS 1.3 push to FedNow / RTP switch. (SLA: < 50ms)

3. ERROR HANDLING & RETRY QUEUES
Failed transmissions trigger automatic dead-letter queue (DLQ) isolation. Non-fatal timeout errors undergo exponential backoff retries (3 attempts max). Automated reversal entries (pacs.007) are emitted if acknowledgment is not received within 30 seconds.

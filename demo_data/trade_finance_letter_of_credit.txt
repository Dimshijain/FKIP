1. SCOPE & UCP 600 COMPLIANCE
The Trade Finance Module processes Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) and Standby Letters of Credit (SBLC) adhering strictly to ICC UCP 600 rules.

2. LC ISSUANCE STAGES
- Applicant Application & Credit Line Hold Approval.
- Issuing Bank Document Verification (Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice).
- SWIFT MT700 Transmission to Advising / Confirming Bank.
- Beneficiary Document Examination & Disbursement.

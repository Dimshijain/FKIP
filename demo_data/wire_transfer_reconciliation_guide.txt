1. NIGHTLY RECONCILIATION PROCESS
The Wire Reconciliation Service automatically matches outgoing MT103 / pacs.008 payment confirmations against internal ledger debit entries.

2. RECONCILIATION MATCHING ENGINE
- Exact Match: Transaction Amount, Currency, Debtor Account, Creditor Account, and End-to-End Reference match 100%.
- Unmatched Exceptions: Flagged for operator review if timestamp mismatch exceeds 2 hours or currency conversion variance exceeds 0.01%.

3. AUDIT LOGGING & ARCHIVAL
All wire transfer ledger modifications are immutable. Records are signed using SHA-256 hashes and retained in PostgreSQL audit tables for 7 years to comply with OCC and FDIC regulations.

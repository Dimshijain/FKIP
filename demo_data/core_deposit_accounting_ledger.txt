1. GENERAL LEDGER POSTING RULES
The Core Deposit Accounting Ledger enforces strict real-time double-entry bookkeeping: `Sum(Debits) == Sum(Credits)`.

2. ACCOUNT TYPES & BALANCE CALCULATION
- Checking (DDA): Demand Deposit Account supporting immediate debit card and wire clearing.
- Savings (SAV): Interest-bearing account with monthly compounded interest calculation.
- Certificate of Deposit (CD): Fixed-term deposit with penalty rules on premature withdrawal.

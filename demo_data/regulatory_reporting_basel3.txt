CLASSIFICATION: Sample Enterprise Specification

1. COMMON EQUITY TIER 1 (CET1) RATIO
Calculates CET1 Capital divided by Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA). Minimum regulatory threshold enforced is 4.5% + 2.5% Capital Conservation Buffer = 7.0%.

2. RISK-WEIGHTED ASSETS (RWA) FORMULA
Applies risk-weight factors (0% for Sovereign US Treasuries, 20% for Interbank Claims, 50% for Mortgages, 100% for Corporate Loans) across all asset portfolios.

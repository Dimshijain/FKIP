1. REAL-TIME VELOCITY RULES
- Rule F-101: Greater than 3 card purchases within 5 minutes in different geographic locations -> IMPOSSIBLE TRAVEL -> DECLINE & SMS ALERT.
- Rule F-102: ATM withdrawal exceeding $1,000 USD between 1:00 AM and 5:00 AM -> HIGH RISK -> STEP-UP OTP REQUIRED.
- Rule F-103: Online e-commerce purchase exceeding $5,000 USD on new un-paired device -> REQUIRE BIOMETRIC VERIFICATION.
